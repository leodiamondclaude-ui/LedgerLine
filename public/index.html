Ledgerline

Books for every client, in one place.

Ledgerline gives your firm one workspace for all the companies you keep books for. Snap a receipt and it’s read, sorted and added. Cash and profit stay current without the spreadsheet chase.

  • Receipts read for you. Upload photos and check the date, vendor, amount and category before they’re added.
  • Every client in its own tab. Label companies by type and switch between them in one click.
  • Invite staff and clients. Clients see only their own books, so they can add receipts themselves.
  • Reports clients understand. Net profit, gross margin and where the money goes, by month or year.
  • Works on any phone. Add it to the home screen and scan receipts on the spot.
